### Check 3 — Telemetry pipeline: the full stdout chain, and whether anything scrubs **[priority]**

**Result: a fixed, measured-by-hash, entirely unscrubbed path from engine stdout to an off-box endpoint.**

1. **Retention on the CVM disk first.** Every service uses `x-logging-conf` (lines 64-69): `driver: json-file`, `max-size: 100m`, `max-file: 10` — **up to ~1 GB of retained stdout per container, on the CVM's disk**, before anything ships it. If the CRITICAL is ever triggered, that is 1 GB of prompt and completion text at rest per engine, plus whatever the sending queue holds.
2. **`otelcol-contrib`'s `filelog` receiver tails all of it**: `include: [/var/lib/docker/containers/*/*-json.log]` (lines 449-453), mounted `/var/lib/docker/containers:ro` (line 409), running `user: "0:0"` (396).
3. **`transform/docker_metadata` (628-638)** resolves `service.name` / `log.source` from the Docker labels.
4. **`filter/app_logs` (640-649) is the *inverse* of the measured harness's filter** — it drops management containers (`compose-manager`, `compose-manager-launcher`, `otelcol-management`, `otelcol-contrib`, `datadog-agent`) and any record with neither `model` nor `deployment` attribute, and **keeps the app containers: both sglang replicas, the proxy, nginx, dcgm, the registrar.**
5. **Export:** `otlphttp/gateway` -> `endpoint: https://telemetry.infra.near.ai` (676-677), literal, `Authorization: Bearer ${MONITORING_INGEST_TOKEN}`, with a **disk-backed sending queue** at `/var/lib/otelcol-contrib/storage` (684-687) — so log bodies also transit a named volume (`otelcol_app_storage`, 410) on their way out.

**[HIGH — systemic] No scrubbing exists at any hop.** The processor chain for `logs/app` is `memory_limiter, resource, transform/docker_metadata, filter/app_logs, groupbyattrs/docker_service, batch` (line 694). Not one of them redacts, hashes, truncates or pattern-matches a log body. The `com.datadoghq.ad.logs` and `nearai.otel.*` labels are routing metadata (model, deployment, env, host, ip, port, instance) — they *tag* streams, they never select or scrub content. **So the complete chain is: anything either engine prints -> json-file (~1 GB retained) -> filelog -> [no scrubbing] -> `https://telemetry.infra.near.ai`.** Both the engine's CRITICAL and its HIGH terminate exactly here.
