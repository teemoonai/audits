## Addendum 2026-11-19 (orchestrator): this image runs under two configurations, and this page describes one

The body of this page describes the collector config embedded in the measured harness compose: `filter/management_logs` keeps compose-manager, launcher and certbot logs and drops everything else. That statement is true of that configuration and remains so on the three harnesses that still embed this image (measured `e8416a02`, `c900dc73`, `a7de0bba`).

It is not a statement about the image. The same digest is the `otelcol-contrib` service of seventeen model-layer recipe revisions read in the 2026-11-19 drift round (the GLM-5.3, small-models, pack-small-models and DeepSeek files). There the config comes from the recipe, and it is a different one: a `filelog` receiver over `/var/lib/docker/containers`, a `filter/app_logs` processor that DROPS records with no `model` and no `deployment` attribute and records of the management services, and so KEEPS the engine and E2EE-proxy containers' stdout, and an `otlphttp` exporter to `https://telemetry.infra.near.ai` with no body-redaction stage. Read by the orchestrator in `prod/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017-vLLM-DEP8-Abliterated.yaml` @ `d1694166` (lines 1273-1282, 1316, 1332-1335); the recipe pages of this round quote the filter for each file.

What follows: whether message content leaves a host through this image is decided by what the engine and the proxy print, not by this image. The engine and proxy pages of this round state which of their log lines can carry content and under what trigger; the recipe pages carry the result per service. The verdict line above is amended to say which configuration this page covers. The class is unchanged.
