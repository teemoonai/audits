### Verdict

**QUALIFIED PASS, for an image that is declared and not running.** The class call follows the rule settled on the lineage pages and is stated so a reader can disagree: a sink that only a malformed or non-standard client request, or a crash, can trigger is ARMED and does not set LEAKS; LEAKS is kept for content that reaches a sink in the course of requests a standard client makes or output the model itself produces. Under `d4cbe817`'s engine flags and behind `e3a4fbca`, nothing of that second kind was found: request and response logging are off, no patch adds a content sink, the two new patches move Engram rows and LoRA indices inside the process, the media fetch is not exercised because the proxy inlines media, and the token-id history is out of the crash dump (v0.34.0 removed the field altogether). The qualifications are the lineage's: draft tokens in the crash dump, and a parser warning that prints a malformed text part. The page would be LEAKS if the proxy's inlining were turned off or bypassed from inside the CVM, since the retry warning then prints media URLs on ordinary fetch failures. Two things specific to this page weaken it relative to its predecessor: the upstream base is new and was reviewed by re-location and targeted diffs rather than as a full tree read, and nothing here has been observed running.

**Residuals.** The base image's correspondence to `62d4bc3c` beyond the 34 patched files rests on a builder-asserted label; native components were not read; enclave egress policy and telemetry retention are outside source.
